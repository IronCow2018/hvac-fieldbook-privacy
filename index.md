# Privacy Policy — HVAC Fieldbook

**Effective Date: November 10, 2026**

HVAC Fieldbook ("the App") is a professional HVAC/R field-service toolkit designed for technicians and service professionals.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled by the App, how that information is stored, and what choices are available to users.

## 1. Information We Handle

HVAC Fieldbook is designed with a local-first approach.

Depending on the features you use, information you enter into the App may include:

- Customer names and contact details
- Customer addresses and service locations
- Equipment names, models, serial numbers, and technical details
- Service job records and maintenance history
- Inspection notes and technician comments
- Parts and labor information
- Photos and attachments added to service records
- Company profile information and technician signatures

This information is provided by you when using the App.

## 2. Local Data Storage

Information entered into HVAC Fieldbook is stored locally on your device.

The App does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

We do not operate a cloud account system or a developer-managed backend for collecting or storing your customer, equipment, or service records.

Your records remain under your control on your device. You are responsible for protecting your device and any files you export or share.

## 3. Data Collection and Sharing

HVAC Fieldbook does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers.

The App does not include third-party advertising or behavioral tracking services.

We do not transmit your locally stored customer, equipment, or service records to our servers.

If you choose to export a report, create a backup, or share a file, the information contained in that file may be provided to the destination you select through the system sharing interface. The privacy practices of that destination are governed by its own provider.

## 4. In-App Purchases

HVAC Fieldbook may offer a one-time Pro unlock through Apple's In-App Purchase system.

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Apple may process transaction and purchase-related information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

You can review Apple's privacy information at:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 5. Device Permissions

The App may request access to certain device features when you choose to use related functionality.

For example:

- Photo Library: to select photos or images for service records.
- Camera: if camera capture is available and you choose to take a photo.

Permissions are requested only when needed for the relevant feature. You can manage app permissions through your device settings.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App.

You can delete individual records using the available in-app controls.

If you uninstall the App, locally stored application data may be removed by the operating system. Files that you previously exported or saved outside the App may remain in their chosen locations and must be deleted separately.

We do not maintain a developer-operated server containing your customer or service records.

## 7. Children's Privacy

HVAC Fieldbook is a professional utility intended for HVAC/R technicians and service professionals. It is not designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Security

We use the platform's local storage capabilities to manage information within the App.

You should keep your device protected with an appropriate passcode or other available security controls. You should also take care when exporting or sharing reports that may contain customer information.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The App does not include third-party advertising networks or analytics SDKs as part of its planned Version 1.0 functionality.

Apple's operating system and App Store services may process information necessary to provide device, distribution, and purchase functionality under Apple's own policies.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The updated version will be published at the same policy URL with a revised effective date.
